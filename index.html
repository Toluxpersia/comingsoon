How it works

How to document: Everything here is built around one unit, a source and the notes you wrote from it.

1

Add the source

The book, video or paper you're learning from.

2

Set a publishing cadence

How often you intend to post notes on it. Weekly, biweekly or monthly.

3

Publish your notes

A chapter, a timestamp, a section at a time.

4

Build an audience

Others subscribe to the source and read along.

Not a notes app. Not a blog.

Organized by source, not by folder

Your notes live under the book, video or paper they came from. Add a book, paper or video and we retrieve the info.

Public by default

Private notes rot in an app you stop opening. Notes written for a reader get written properly.

People subscribe to a source, not to you

Someone working through the same book, video or paper can follow your notes on it specifically.

No feed to fall into

Nothing is pushed at you.
