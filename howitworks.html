How thinking-ape works

thinking-ape turns passive consumption into active thinking. You add a source — a book, video or paper — pick how often you'll write, then write to think about what you learn, chapter by chapter, timestamp by timestamp, section by section. Others can subscribe and follow along.

Getting started

thinking-ape uses passwordless sign-in — a one-time 6-digit code sent to your email. There's nothing to remember.

Create an account

  1. On the landing page, open the Sign in / Sign up box and choose Sign Up.
  2. Enter your full name, email, and a username.
  3. We email you a 6-digit code — enter it to verify.
  4. You're in. Your session stays signed in on this device.

⚠️ Your full name and username are permanent — they can't be changed later, so choose carefully.

Sign in

  1. Open the Sign in / Sign up box.
  2. Enter the email you signed up with.
  3. We send a fresh 6-digit code to that email.
  4. Enter the code to sign in.

For your privacy, the code screen looks the same whether or not an account exists for that email.

Add a new source

A source is the thing you're thinking about: a book, a video, or a paper. Click New Source and pick a type. Every type needs a title, at least one author, a year, one or more subject tags, and a writing frequency.

📖 Book

  1. Choose Book.
  2. Search the book by title — results come from Open Library, with covers.
  3. Select a book to auto-fill its title, authors, year and cover. Can't find it? Choose the manual option and enter the details by hand.
  4. Confirm or fill the required fields and add subject tags.
  5. Pick a writing frequency and create it.

You don't upload a table of contents. After the book is created, you add each chapter/section manually as you write (see Writing & publishing).

🎥 Video

  1. Choose Video.
  2. Paste the video link and click Extract — the title, authors, year and keywords are pulled in automatically. No result? Enter them by hand.
  3. Confirm the required fields and add subject tags.
  4. Upload a source thumbnail.
  5. Pick a writing frequency and create it.

📄 Paper

  1. Choose Paper.
  2. Paste the paper link or DOI and click Extract — the title, authors, year, abstract and keywords are pulled in automatically. No result? Enter them by hand.
  3. Confirm the required fields and add subject tags.
  4. Upload a source thumbnail.
  5. Pick a writing frequency and create it.

Writing frequency & schedule

When you create a source you choose how often you intend to write about it. thinking-ape then tracks a next-post-due date and keeps you honest.

Weekly
Biweekly
Monthly

Staying on schedule

  • Each source shows its next post due date.
  • We send you a reminder before a deadline.
  • Publish a post and the schedule rolls forward to the next date.

If you miss a deadline

  • The source is flagged as missed and you get a heads-up email.
  • A post published after its deadline is marked late — late posts can't be upvoted.
  • Need a break? You can pause a source until a date you choose.

Writing & publishing

This is the heart of it: writing to think. Open one of your sources and add an entry for whatever you just finished — a chapter, a timestamp, a section.

Write a post

  1. Open your source, or click write/think and pick the source.
  2. Give the entry a title — e.g. “Chapter 3”, “Timestamp 12:40”, or a section name.
  3. Write your thinking in the editor (rich text).
  4. Optionally pick a reading format/theme.
  5. Publish — it's added to the source and pushed to your subscribers' feeds.

✅ When you've written everything you meant to, hit Finish on the source to mark it complete (100%).

Subscribing & your feed

Follow other thinkers

  • Subscribe to any source to follow along.
  • New posts from your subscriptions collect in your feed.
  • Unsubscribe anytime.

Discover

  • Use search to find people, sources and topics.
  • Browse a user's profile to see everything they're thinking about.

Upvotes, bookmarks & notifications

Upvote

Bookmark

Save any post to your bookmarks to come back to it later.

Notifications

Get notified about new posts from your subscriptions, new subscribers, upvotes and bookmarks — in real time.

Your profile & account

Your profile

  • Your public profile shows your sources and posts.
  • You can edit your profile picture, bio and location.
  • Edit or delete your own posts and sources anytime.

The permanent bits

  • Your username and full name are set once at sign-up and cannot be changed.
  • Sign-in is by email code — keep access to your email.
8

Good to know

Do I have to finish a source?

No. Write at your own pace on the frequency you chose. When you're done, hit Finish to mark it complete — or just stop.

Can I write on my phone?

You can read and browse everywhere. Writing a post is best on a larger screen — the editor prompts you to switch to desktop on mobile.

What if the extractor can't read my video or paper link?

Just choose the manual option and type the title, authors and year yourself.

What happens if I miss my writing deadline?

The source is flagged, you get an email, and any post you publish afterward is marked late (and can't be upvoted). Pause the source if you need a real break.
